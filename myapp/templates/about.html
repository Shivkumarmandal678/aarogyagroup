About Aarogya Group

Helping workers leave prepared, informed and confident.

Leadership at Aarogya

A Message from our Chairman

Our responsibility is to help every worker begin an overseas journey with knowledge, dignity, and confidence.

Chairman

Aarogya Group

“Every person who comes to Aarogya brings a hope for a better future. We believe that hope deserves honest guidance, careful preparation, and respectful support.”

We are committed to making medical biometric orientation, pre-departure training, document guidance, and travel preparation easier to understand. Our team works so that workers and their families can make informed decisions before the journey begins.

Chairman's Advice to Every Worker:
  • Ask questions and understand each step before signing or travelling.
  • Keep your passport, medical reports, receipts, and contact details secure.
  • Follow medical and biometric instructions carefully and arrive on time.
  • Respect local laws, workplace safety rules, and the culture of your destination.
